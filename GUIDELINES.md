# Guidelines and Mechanics

**HACK ILOILO: THE 2026 ILOILO PROVINCE STARTUP HACKATHON**

## Rationale

The Iloilo Provincial Government (IPG), through Provincial Ordinance No. 2025-332 or the Iloilo Startup Development Ordinance of 2024, institutionalized startup development in the province. The Iloilo Province Startup Development Committee (IPSDC), with the Office of the Local Economic Development and Investment Promotion Officer (OLEDIPO) as Secretariat, leads its implementation. The Ordinance mandates the IPSDC to design startup competitions and supports startup development activities, including hackathons.

Building on the 2025 hackathon, the 2nd Iloilo Province Startup Hackathon expands participation beyond students and focuses on developing working prototypes that address pressing challenges in the province, particularly in the IPG and IPSDC priority sectors of Tourism, Agriculture, and Services. Teams are expected to develop their solutions in collaboration with an identified potential early adopter.

The hackathon serves as an entry point to the province’s startup development pipeline. It focuses on generating and validating ideas and developing initial prototypes, while the IPSDC Grant supports existing MVP-stage solutions ready for further development and validation. Through this pathway, hackathon teams and prototypes may progress toward MVP development, market validation, and potential grant support.

## Eligibility and Pre-Qualification

1. **General Eligibility**
   The hackathon is open to individuals who are **based in, studying in, working in, or actively serving Iloilo Province**.

All participants must:

- Be at least 18 years old at the time of registration;
- Have a direct connection to Iloilo Province through residence,RATI education, employment, business, community work, or other professional or civic engagement;
- Have no final conviction involving a labor, civil, administrative, or criminal case that may affect their eligibility to participate;
- Have no relationship within the fourth degree of consanguinity with any member of the screening or judging panel; and
- Agree to and sign the required Participant Agreement, including provisions on confidentiality/NDA, intellectual property, and use of prizes or incentives.

2. **Entry Mode and Team Composition**

Participants may join the hackathon as a team consisting of three (3) to five (5) members.

Each team must have:

- A designated Team Lead;
- At least one member responsible for Build/Technology;
- At least one member responsible for Business/Commercialization; and
- At least one member with expertise in Design/Communications or relevant Domain Expertise.

A member may fulfill more than one role, provided that the team collectively demonstrates the required competencies.

3. **Team Diversity**

Teams are encouraged to form multidisciplinary and complementary teams, bringing together technical, business, creative, and sector-specific expertise relevant to their proposed solution.

## Hackathon Process

The hackathon will follow a structured process from problem identification to solution development and final pitching.

1. **Reverse Pitching**

- Problem statements or case studies will be presented by participating Departments/Units of the Iloilo Provincial Government.
- Teams will review the identified challenges and select one problem to address.
- Teams are expected to clearly understand the needs, context, and intended users associated with their selected problem.

2. **Ideation & Concept Generation**
   Teams will develop and refine innovative solution concepts addressing their selected problem. Teams should validate their initial assumptions through available information, stakeholder inputs, and, where feasible, interaction with potential users or early adopters.
3. **Product Development & Prototyping**
   Teams will translate their solution concept into a **technology-enabled functional prototype** within the hackathon period. The prototype should demonstrate the core features and value of the proposed solution and provide sufficient evidence that the concept can potentially address the identified problem.
4. **Pitch Deck Preparation**
   Teams will prepare and deliver a concise pitch presenting the problem, proposed solution, target users or early adopter, prototype, value proposition, potential impact, and pathway toward further development.

## Mechanics for Prototype Development

- Each team must develop a **technology-enabled functional prototype** that directly addresses the selected problem statement or case.
- The prototype must be developed within the official hackathon period and must demonstrate, at minimum:
  - Functionality – The prototype demonstrates the core features or workflow of the proposed solution.
  - User Experience – The prototype provides a simple, intuitive, and accessible experience appropriate to its intended users.
  - Innovation – The solution introduces a meaningful improvement, new approach, or distinctive feature in addressing the identified problem.
  - Problem-Solution Fit – The prototype demonstrates a clear connection between the identified problem, the proposed solution, and the needs of its intended users.
- Teams may use any appropriate programming languages, development tools, platforms, frameworks, APIs, or other technologies at their discretion, subject to applicable licensing and intellectual property requirements.
- The prototype must be the **original work of the participating team** and must be substantially developed during the official hackathon period. Pre-existing products, applications, or substantially completed projects may not be submitted as the team's prototype.
- Teams may use publicly available tools, libraries, APIs, datasets, and generative AI tools, provided that their use complies with applicable licenses, terms of service, and intellectual property requirements. Teams remain responsible for the originality, accuracy, functionality, and ownership of their submitted work.
- Regular check-ins will be conducted to monitor progress and ensure active development.

- The prototype must be **demonstrable during the final presentation**. Teams may use a live demonstration, working software, clickable prototype, hardware demonstration, simulation, or other appropriate means to demonstrate how the proposed solution works.

## Mechanics for Presentation & Demo

Each team will present their solution to the panel of judges through a pitch presentation and live demo of their MVP.

Pitch Deck must clearly cover:

- Problem Statement
- Proposed Solution & Unique Value Proposition
- Demonstration of MVP/Prototype
- Implementation Plan & Potential Impact
- Scalability and Sustainability
- Live Demo of the MVP must showcase its core functionality.

  Time Allocation:

- 5–7 minutes for presentation & demo
- 10 minutes for Q\&A with the judges

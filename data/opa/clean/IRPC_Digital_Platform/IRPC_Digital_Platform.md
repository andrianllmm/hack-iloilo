## Today, every handoff creates friction

No advance slots or prices · Manual consolidation · Delayed cost visibility · No live queue

The result: decisions arrive after the operational moment has passed.

## Rules the platform must respect

| PRICES      | Posted on the office board and weekly on Facebook        |
|-------------|----------------------------------------------------------|
| PRICE BASIS | Market price now; NFA price once IRPC is an LEE          |
| VALIDATION  | Farmer confirms at weigh-in and again after drying       |
| COST VIEW   | Energy and margin seen only after month-end books        |
| RECEIPTS    | Audit-ready records that never replace official receipts |

"A two-sided digital platform that streamlines operations, improves transparency, and maximizes IRPC's utilization year-round."

Fill idle capacity Speed procurement Trace every batch Lower cost per kilo Sustain subsidy surpluses
